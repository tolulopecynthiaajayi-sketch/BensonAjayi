Genesis & Parentage
I

Born into Love & Faith

Born into a loving home on the 24th of October 1966 to Mrs. Olufunke Esther Ajayi and Mr. Isaac Akinola Ajayi. From early childhood, his upbringing was steeped in strong moral principles, humility, godliness, and a spirit of diligence that formed the cornerstone of his life.

A Blessed Home & Sanctuary

Happily married to his beloved life partner and jewel, Mrs. Ololade Hannah Benson-Ajayi. Their fruitful union is blessed with four wonderful children, creating a warm, faith-filled sanctuary of unconditional love, prayer, laughter, and generational favor.

Born into a loving home on the 24th of October 1966 to Mrs. Olufunke Esther Ajayi and Mr. Isaac Akinola Ajayi. He schooled in Bishop Philips Primary School, St. Ambrose's Grammar School, and St. Joseph Secondary School. He advanced through the Federal Polytechnic Ado-Ekiti, then Obafemi Awolowo University. His corporate journey traversed Michael Stevens, UAC, NCR, ARM, LCC, and Mixta Africa. Married to Ololade Hannah Benson-Ajayi, they are blessed with 4 children.
